M1 Artificial Intelligence · semester 7 · Choco

Choco

Eleven Caseine exercises in constraint programming, solved with pychoco: puzzles and combinatorial problems, from magic squares and N-queens to car sequencing and steel production.

Puzzles and combinatorial problems

Each card opens that exercise's step by step.

01 Toy meeting Scheduling a toy meeting under time and resource constraints. 02 Magic square A magic square, filled so every row, column and diagonal matches. 03 ABCDE × 4 Finding digits A–E such that ABCDE × 4 = EDCBA. 04 Birthday puzzle Guessing a birth year from a handful of clues. 05 Sujiko A Sujiko puzzle: numbers placed in a grid to match the corner sums. 06 Heterosquare A heterosquare puzzle, where every row, column and diagonal sum differs. 07 Magic series A magic series, the self-referential counterpart to the magic square. 08 N-queens The classic N-queens puzzle, placing queens so that none attacks another. 09 Warehouse location The warehouse location problem: which warehouses to open to serve demand. 10 Car sequencing Car sequencing on an assembly line, across several instance sizes. 11 Steel production Steel production planning, across several instance sizes.

How the work is recorded

Each exercise that gets solved carries a steps page: what it asks, and once it is solved, what was tried, what was rejected, and the reference that settled it. A choice without a reference is a guess, so the trail is written down where the code can be read beside it.
