# Master of Artificial Intelligence

> [Lire en français](#version-francaise)

**Programme:** [Master of Artificial Intelligence](https://m-ai.imag.fr/),
Université Grenoble Alpes — jointly delivered by **UFR IM²AG** (Informatics and
Mathematics) and **Ensimag** (Grenoble INP).

**Structure:** two years. M1 covers semesters S7–S8; M2 is one semester of
theory (S9) plus an internship of at least 18 weeks (S10), in industry or a
public research laboratory. M2 splits into two tracks — *Foundations and
Advanced Methods in Machine Learning*, and *Applied Artificial Intelligence and
Interactive Systems*.

**Status:** M1 in progress. Coursework will be added here as it is produced.

Meanwhile the applied machine-learning and statistics work from the Licence is
under [Bachelor/](../Bachelor/) — see the [repository README](../README.md) for
the projects worth starting with.

## Master

**Formation :** [Master of Artificial Intelligence](https://m-ai.imag.fr/),
Université Grenoble Alpes — co-porté par l'**UFR IM²AG** (informatique et
mathématiques) et **Ensimag** (Grenoble INP).
