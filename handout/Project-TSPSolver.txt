2.5     Performance evaluation
You are expected to evaluate and compare the performance of the implemented
algorithms. This can take various forms, such as:
    • Measuring the execution time of each algorithm on different instances.
    • Comparing the quality of the solutions obtained by each algorithm (i.e.,
      the length of the tour).
    • Analyzing the scalability of each algorithm as the size of the instances
      increases.
    • Analyzing the behavior of the algorithms on specific instances using visu-
      alizations tools.
You can use the provided instances and also create your own instances to
test the algorithms under different conditions. We provide a Python script
instance_generator.py that generates random TSP instances. Feel free to
modify it to create instances that suit your testing needs.
    The results of your performance evaluation should be documented in a re-
port or a notebook, including any relevant figures, graphs or tables to illustrate
your findings.
