# Lab2: Convergence and estimation in the exponential model

Three simulation studies in R: how sample averages settle down and take on a
bell shape as sample size grows, checked across four different underlying
models; two estimators of an exponential rate compared by empirical risk; and
a confidence interval whose tail split is tuned numerically.
